# AI-System-to-Automatically-Review-and-Summarize-Research-Papers-

This project is an AI-powered  system designed to automatically review and summarize research papers, helping researchers and students quickly understand the core ideas of research paper. The system accepts research papers as input, extracts and analyzes their content, and generates concise summaries along with structured insights such as abstracts and the summary of the entire research  .  By automating the paper review process, this project reduces the time and effort required for manual analysis and improves efficiency in literature review and academic research workflows.
